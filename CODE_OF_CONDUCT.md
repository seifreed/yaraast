# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Contributors must make participation respectful and harassment-free. Welcome
constructive technical disagreement, give actionable feedback, respect privacy,
and avoid discriminatory, threatening, sexualized, or personally insulting
conduct.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately through
[GitHub private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/seifreed/yaraast/security/advisories/new)
with `Code of Conduct` in the title. Maintainers may edit or remove content and
temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Reports are handled as
confidentially as practical and enforcement decisions are based on impact,
context, and repeated behavior.

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project in
public spaces.
